Upstream and Downstream Traceability

What is upstream and downstream traceability?

Upstream and downstream traceability is the ability to trace a product backward, to its raw materials and suppliers, and forward, to its destination and customers. Together they cover the product's entire journey through the supply chain.

Both are essential for executing targeted recalls and responding quickly to an issue.
