Batch coding / lot numbering

What is batch coding?

Batch coding is the assignment of a unique identifier to each group of product manufactured under uniform conditions. That code links the product to its raw materials, its manufacturing date and its quality controls.

It's the foundation of upstream and downstream traceability and of shelf-life control.
