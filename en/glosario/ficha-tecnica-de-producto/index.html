Product Technical Data Sheet

What is a product technical data sheet?

A product technical data sheet is the document that records a food product's specifications: composition, physicochemical and sensory characteristics, allergens, storage conditions and shelf life. It defines, in a standardized way, what the product must meet.

It's a key reference for customers, audits, labeling and quality control during goods receipt and release.
