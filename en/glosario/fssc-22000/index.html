FSSC 22000

What is FSSC 22000 certification?

FSSC 22000 certification is a GFSI-recognized food safety scheme that combines the ISO 22000 standard, sector-specific prerequisite programs (ISO/TS 22002) and additional requirements of its own. It offers a complete, auditable management system.

It's widely adopted in the food manufacturing industry for its management-system approach and international recognition.
