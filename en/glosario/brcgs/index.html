BRCGS

What is BRCGS certification?

BRCGS (Brand Reputation Compliance Global Standards) certification is an international food safety scheme recognized by GFSI that evaluates a plant's management system, product control and manufacturing practices. It's widely required by the UK market and retailers.

It helps manufacturers reach customers who demand audited guarantees of safety and quality.
