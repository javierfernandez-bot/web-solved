Non-quality indicators

What are non-quality indicators?

Non-quality indicators are the metrics that quantify the cost of quality failures, such as rework, waste, complaints, returns or rejected product. They translate quality problems into economic terms.

Measuring them turns quality into a business case and helps prioritize where to invest in improvement.
