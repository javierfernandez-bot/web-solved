Product Recall

What is a product recall?

A product recall is the recovery of items already distributed on the market due to a food safety risk or a specification non-conformity. Its goal is to prevent the affected product from reaching or remaining with the consumer.

Solid traceability enables fast, targeted recalls with less economic and reputational impact.
