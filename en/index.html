Frequently asked questions

What is Solved?

Solved is factory software that centralizes quality control, issue management and continuous improvement in a single platform, accessible from mobile, tablet and PC.

Which companies is Solved built for?

For factories and industries — food, chemical, packaging, general manufacturing — that want to digitize checklists, audits and issues and eliminate paper.

Does Solved integrate with my ERP, MES or CMMS?

Yes. Solved connects with the leading ERP, MES and CMMS systems on the market to avoid duplicate data and centralize management.

Can I use Solved from my phone?

Yes. Solved works on mobile, tablet and PC, so your team can log and check information from the production floor or on the move.

How long does implementation take?

Average implementation time for Solved is 2 to 3 weeks, with support throughout the entire rollout process.
