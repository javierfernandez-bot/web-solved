Quality record

What is a quality record?

A quality record is the document or data that evidences that a check, parameter or procedure has been carried out as established. It constitutes the objective proof of compliance in the plant and is essential for passing audits and certifications.

Digitising records removes the paper, speeds up audits and turns the captured data into real-time indicators.
