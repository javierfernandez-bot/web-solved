Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between an issue and a corrective action?

The issue flags the problem; the corrective action resolves it. Every issue or audit finding generates an action plan with an owner, a closing date and tracking through to resolution.

How is an owner and a date assigned to each action?

When you create the action, you choose an owner and a closing date. That person gets an instant notification by app and email, and Solved sends reminders before the deadline.

Does Solved manage audit non-conformities (ISO 9001, IFS, BRC)?

Yes. It logs every audit or inspection non-conformity, assigns it with an owner and a deadline, and keeps full traceability from detection to closure. Compatible with ISO 9001, IFS, BRC, ISO 22000 and HACCP.

What is root cause analysis and what is it for?

It's a lean methodology for getting to the real source of a problem, not just the symptom. Solved links corrective actions to the analysis so the fix is permanent and the problem doesn't repeat.

Can I schedule planned tasks in addition to corrective actions?

Yes. Alongside reactive work, you can schedule recurring or one-off tasks and assign them to departments or people, with the same tracking as corrective actions.
