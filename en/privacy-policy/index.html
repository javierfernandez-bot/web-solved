2. Scope: when we act as controller and when as processor

Solved acts as data controller for website data, sales contacts, the management of its customers' accounts, support and usage analytics of the platform.

The data each customer company enters into the platform (incidents, records, checklists, documents, assets and its employees' data) belongs to the customer, which is its controller. Solved processes it solely as a data processor, on the customer's behalf and under the Article 28 GDPR agreement signed with it. If your company uses Solved and you wish to exercise your rights over that data, please contact your company; Solved will provide the necessary assistance.

3. Information we collect

  • Contact details: first name, surname, company, role, phone and email.

  • Data supplied through forms: information you voluntarily give us when requesting a demo, contact or subscription.

  • Platform account data: name, email address, job title, company, assigned plants and departments, language and, if two-step verification is enabled, phone number.

  • Platform usage data: screens visited, actions performed, last sign-in date, device and browser, and technical error logs.

  • Support messages: what the user writes in the support assistant, together with their name, email address and any screenshots they attach.

  • Website browsing data: IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited and browsing time.

  • Cookies: we use our own and third-party cookies to improve the browsing experience and gather usage statistics. You can find more information in our Cookie Policy.

5. Data retention

  • Sales contacts and customer data: for as long as the relationship lasts and, afterwards, for the periods needed to meet legal obligations or handle possible claims.

  • Users removed from the platform: their account is permanently deleted after 7 days.

  • Items deleted by the customer (incidents, actions, checklists, templates): permanently purged after 3 months.

  • Technical and system activity logs: automatically deleted after 90 days.

  • Backups: automatically expire within 30 days at most.

  • Customer data when the service ends: can be exported for 30 days and are then deleted, unless there is a legal obligation to retain them.

7. International transfers

When a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, the transfer relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and, where the provider is certified, on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF).

8. Artificial intelligence

The platform's artificial intelligence features process data in the European Union, using Mistral models hosted on Google Cloud. Providers with processing in the USA (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini) are only used if the customer company authorises it in writing. Each customer company can turn these features off.

Solved does not use its customers' data to train artificial intelligence models, whether its own or third parties'. AI providers process the data solely to generate the requested response and, under their contractual terms, do not use it to train their models.

9. Your rights

You may exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Access to your personal data.

  • Rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data.

  • Erasure (“right to be forgotten”).

  • Restriction of processing.

  • Portability of your data.

  • Objection to processing or withdrawal of consent.

To exercise them, write to info@trysolved.com, stating the right you wish to exercise and including proof of your identity. For data your company has entered into the platform, please contact your company, which is the controller of that data.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) if you believe your rights have not been respected: www.aepd.es.

10. Information security

Solved applies appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, alteration or destruction. They include:

  • Encryption of communications (TLS) and of data at rest.

  • Logical separation of each customer company's data, checked on every access.

  • Access only for users created by their company, with roles and permissions, and Google or Microsoft sign-in and two-step verification available.

  • Daily backups and point-in-time recovery, with regular restore tests.

  • Notification to the customer, within 36 hours of becoming aware, of any security breach affecting personal data processed on its behalf.

However, no system is completely secure, so absolute security of information cannot be guaranteed.
