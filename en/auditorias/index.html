Frequently asked questions

What is a digital checklist?

It's a checklist your team fills out from mobile, tablet or PC instead of on paper, with automatic logging of date, owner and results in real time.

Can I customize checklists for each process?

Yes. Solved lets you create custom templates for every control, inspection or procedure, tailored to your standards and how you work.

Does Solved help with IFS, BRC or ISO 9001 compliance?

Yes. With digital records always available and up to date, you simplify regulatory compliance and save time on audits and certifications.

Can I schedule the frequency of each control?

Yes. You set the frequency and date for each procedure, and Solved sends alerts and assigns owners so nothing gets missed.

Does it work on any device?

Yes. You can fill out and review your records from mobile, tablet or PC, wherever you are.
