Case study · Potato packing

An hour, or two, walking the plant with an Excel sheet, for the monthly general checklist.

Jose Vicente Doria heads Quality and Environment at Patatas Aguilar, a Valencian company with more than 50 years selecting and packing potatoes. He explains how they used to run the monthly checklist, why another system they tried before Solved didn't work for them, what changed from 2024, and what he tells a manager who is still on the fence.
