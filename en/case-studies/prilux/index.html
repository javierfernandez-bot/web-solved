Case study · Lighting

More than a thousand inspections a year, logged on paper to begin with.

Álvaro Sobrino runs product quality at Prilux, a lighting group with its own factory and four very different business units. In two minutes he explains the move from paper to spreadsheet and from there to Solved in 2024, why the main selection criterion was adoption across the whole organisation — from the shop-floor operator to management — and the results of the first months.
