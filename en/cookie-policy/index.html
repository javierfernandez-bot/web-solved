What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. Other technologies, such as the browser's local storage, serve similar purposes. In this policy we refer to all of them as cookies.

How to manage cookies

The first time you visit the site you will see a notice with two options. If you click Accept, the Google Maps map in the footer is loaded. If you click Reject, the map is not loaded and a link to open it in Google Maps is shown instead. To change your choice, clear this site's data in your browser and the notice will appear again.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You will find more information at www.allaboutcookies.org.
