Specific applications

Solved applications in a food plant, with their respective scope.

HACCP: critical control points, prerequisites and verifications logged on the floor, with time, owner and evidence.

ISO 22000: the evidence the standard asks for, produced on the floor — records, prerequisites, non-conformities and internal audit.

Certification software: IFS, BRCGS, ISO 9001 and FSSC 22000 on the same records and the same traceability, done once.

Supplier approval: certificates, spec sheets and goods-in incidents in one file per supplier. It is the document manager, not a separate module.

Batch traceability: each batch is linked to its checks, its incidents and its actions, from raw material to finished product.

Internal audits: calendar, checklist and evidence on the records already generated at the plant, with no preparation needed before the visit.

Frequently asked questions

Is Solved suitable for HACCP?

Yes. It digitises HACCP and its prerequisites, with traceable records available at any moment for audits and inspections.

Does it help pass IFS or BRC audits?

Yes. It keeps quality and food safety records current and available, which cuts the work before certification.

Does it enable traceability on the floor?

Yes. The system records checks, batches and verifications, which secures traceability and compliance right through the process.

Does it get paper out of the food plant?

Yes. Everything is logged from a phone or tablet, which removes paper and transcription errors.
